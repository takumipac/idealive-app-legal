CaptionCue Support

FAQ

Q. Captions aren't generated.
A. CaptionCue uses on-device speech recognition (English, US). Make sure English dictation is available on your device (Settings → General → Keyboard → Enable Dictation). The first run may take a few minutes while the model downloads. v1 supports English audio only — more languages are coming.

Q. Is my video uploaded anywhere?
A. No. Everything runs on your iPhone. Your video never leaves your device.

Q. How do I restore my purchase?
A. Tap "Restore Purchases" in Settings or on the paywall.

Q. How do I cancel my subscription?
A. iPhone Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions.
