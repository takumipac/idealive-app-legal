CaptionCue Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 1, 2026

Data Collection

CaptionCue - Auto Subtitles ("the App") does not collect any personal information or usage data. The App contains no analytics or advertising SDKs.

Your Videos and Audio

All video processing and speech recognition happen entirely on your device using Apple's on-device speech recognition. Your videos, audio, and captions are never uploaded to any server. Exported videos are saved only where you choose.

Purchases

Pro purchases (subscriptions and lifetime unlock) are processed by Apple through the App Store. The developer never receives your payment details.
