Loop Support

Loop tracks the things you haven't closed yet: what you owe people, what people owe you, and money at risk. Your to-do list ends when you hit send. Loop starts there. Everything runs on your iPhone, with no account required.

Frequently asked questions

Q. How do I add a loop from another app?
A. Select the text or screenshot in Notes, Photos, Messages, Mail, Safari, or any app with a Share button, tap Share, and choose Loop. The item appears in Loop's review queue so you can check it and save it. You can also type, speak, or pick a screenshot from inside Loop.

Q. How does AI extraction work?
A. Loop reads what you captured and suggests the person, what it is about, the date, the amount, and whether it is something you owe, something you are waiting on, or money at risk. On supported devices this uses Apple Intelligence on-device models. Otherwise, Loop uses a built-in on-device parser. Either way, nothing leaves your iPhone, and you can edit every suggestion before saving.

Q. Does Loop send messages for me?
A. No. The follow-up composer drafts a message in the tone you pick, and you copy or send it yourself.

Q. How do subscriptions work?
A. Loop is free to use with limits on active loops and monthly smart captures. Loop Pro removes those limits and unlocks additional features. The annual plan includes a 14-day free trial. Subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period.

Q. How do I restore my purchase?
A. Open Settings in Loop and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID used for the original purchase.

Q. How do I cancel my subscription?
A. On your iPhone, open Settings > your name > Subscriptions, choose Loop, and tap Cancel Subscription.

Q. How do I delete my data?
A. In Loop, go to Settings > Delete all data. This removes all loops, captures, and stored files from your device and cancels all pending notifications.
