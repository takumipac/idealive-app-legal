Loop Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 30, 2026

Developer: Takumi Matsuda (idealive)

Data collection

Loop (the "App") does not collect any personal information or usage data. The App contains no analytics SDKs and no advertising SDKs, requires no account, and has no servers of its own. The developer never receives your data.

Data stored on your device

Everything you enter or capture, including loops, names and contacts, amounts, captured text, and optional screenshots, is stored only on your device. Nothing is transmitted to the developer or to any third party. If you choose to keep the original image of a screenshot, it also stays on your device.

On-device processing (AI, OCR, speech)

Extracting loops from text (using Apple Intelligence on-device models where available, or a built-in on-device rule-based parser otherwise), recognizing text in images (OCR), and transcribing speech are all performed on your iPhone. Your text, images, and audio are never sent to an external server. Any speech-model downloads are performed by iOS from Apple.

Permissions and their purpose

Microphone and Speech Recognition: to let you add a loop by voice. Audio is transcribed on your iPhone.
Notifications: for your daily digest, follow-up reminders, and money deadline reminders. All notifications are created locally on your device.
Photos: only the images you choose are read, to recognize text in them.

Sharing and follow-up messages

Sharing content into Loop from other apps, and copying or sending a follow-up message, happen only through actions you take yourself. Loop never sends messages on your behalf.

Purchases

Subscription purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. The developer does not receive your payment information.

Deleting your data

You can erase all data stored by the App at any time in Settings > Delete all data inside the app, or by deleting the App from your device.

Children

Loop is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
